About Everstone Holdings

A foundation-first approach to building a stable real estate portfolio.

Everstone Holdings, LLC is a developing real estate investment company committed to long-term ownership of high-quality rental homes and income-producing assets. Our approach centers around disciplined acquisitions and sustainable growth.

Over the next several years, Everstone is focused on establishing its core foundation — prioritizing market stability, rental demand, and properties with lasting value.

Investment Focus

Types of properties Everstone is actively seeking.

  • Single-family homes in stable rental markets
  • Duplexes and small multi-family buildings
  • Value-add opportunities needing minor to moderate repairs
  • Homes typically under ~$200k depending on the market
  • Future expansion into small apartments and commercial assets

Acquisition Criteria

What we look for during evaluation.

  • Stable or growing neighborhoods
  • 3 bed / 2 bath homes preferred
  • Strong rental demand with long-term performance potential
  • Light to moderate renovation needs
  • Clear value-add or appreciation pathways
  • Direct-to-seller or off-market opportunities preferred
